description=[h1]Infinite Irrigation Pipes — Farming, Water and Power in One Pipe[/h1]
description=[i]Build 42 crop automation, infinite clean water, whole-building water and native electrical power.[/i]
description=Source: https://github.com/Kymorphius/Infinite-Irrigation-System
description=
description=[h1]Recent Highlights[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Magic Fridges:[/b] Nine refrigerator styles combine million-capacity storage, permanent freshness, water, power and configurable seed-to-crop production.
description=[*] [b]Optional Automatic Farming:[/b] Pipes can sow, harvest and manage per-crop produce and seed targets from their 10000-capacity storage.
description=[*] [b]Independent Services:[/b] Irrigation, plowing, crop care, fertilization, power, water and whole-building water remain separately controllable.
description=[*] [b]Safety and Convenience:[/b] Includes farmland protection, old-furrow repair, selectable 1x1–15x15 ranges, clearer menus and map-based pipe location.
description=[*] [b]Optional Area Cleanup:[/b] A hidden-by-default switch can remove selected vegetation, trees, zombies, corpses, blood and ground items.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Quick Start[/h1]
description=[list=1]
description=[*] Craft [b]16 Infinite Pipes from 1 clean or dirty Ripped Sheet[/b] anywhere. No light, table, workstation or tool is required.
description=[*] Right-click [b]Infinite Pipe[/b], choose Farming, Water or Power directly, then choose a Cross, Line, Corner or T shape.
description=[*] On a placed pipe, use the [b]Farming[/b] master switch to toggle Irrigation, Auto-Plowing, Crop Care and Fertilization together, or adjust each one independently. Power, Water, Whole-Building Water and range remain separate controls.
description=[*] Switching every service off leaves a reusable inactive floor pipe. Removing it returns the item.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Magic Fridges[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Craft any of [b]9 refrigerator styles from 1 clean or dirty Ripped Sheet[/b] in the dedicated Magic Fridges category.
description=[*] Each placed fridge has [b]1,000,000 logical capacity[/b], keeps every stored item permanently fresh, supplies infinite clean water for drinking/filling/cleaning, provides native power and can supply its whole building.
description=[*] Recognized seeds automatically produce their crop. The cycle is configurable and defaults to [b]10 minutes[/b]. Seeds are preserved by default; Sandbox or Mod Options can make each cycle consume one seed.
description=[*] Open the fridge and use [b]Production Settings[/b] to enable crops and set an exact stock limit per crop; blank means unlimited. [b]Save to All Magic Fridges[/b] applies the current profile world-wide.
description=[*] Pick up a Magic Fridge without emptying it: its contents and production settings travel with it and return when placed again.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Configurable Farming[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Smart Irrigation:[/b] Maintains a suitable water level for each crop rather than blindly setting every plant to 100.
description=[*] [b]Auto-Sow and Auto-Harvest:[/b] Empty plowed tiles are planted from the pipe inventory. Ripe crops are harvested back into the same pipe, then can be tilled and replanted automatically.
description=[*] [b]Stock Targets, Not Hard Caps:[/b] The default target is 9 produce and 9 seeds per crop. Below target, one plant's complete native yield is kept, so stock may slightly exceed the target; at or above target, remaining ripe plants stay in the field until stock is removed.
description=[*] [b]Bulk and Per-Crop Settings:[/b] Use the Settings button at the bottom of the open pipe inventory to enable crops, keep harvested seeds, edit individual targets or apply one value to all crops. Standard farming registrations from other mods appear automatically.
description=[*] [b]Independent Services:[/b] Irrigation, auto-plowing, crop care and fertilization operate independently. Each placed pipe can override the global auto-plowing default.
description=[*] [b]Immediate Response:[/b] Newly seeded and just-harvested crops inside coverage are processed without waiting for the maintenance cycle.
description=[*] [b]Protection and Recovery:[/b] Optional pest and disease removal, free composting, health recovery, mature-crop protection, no-rotting behavior, revival and instant maturity.
description=[*] [b]Harvest XP Ownership Repair:[/b] Ownerless legacy or mod-created crops inherit the nearest pipe owner when cared for; existing vanilla ownership is never overwritten. Ownerless multiplayer pipes can be explicitly claimed.
description=[/list]
description=Farming services require no barrels, rain collectors, pumps, plumbing, electricity or consumable water.
description=
description=[h1]Water Supply and Whole-Building Water[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Enable [b]Water Supply[/b] to drink, fill containers and use cleaning actions directly from the pipe.
description=[*] For ordinary vanilla plumbing, place a supply pipe one floor above an appliance within its upper 3x3 area, hold a Pipe Wrench and use the vanilla plumbing action.
description=[*] Alternatively, enable [b]Whole-Building Water[/b] on a supply pipe inside a building or directly beside an exterior wall. It supplies unlimited clean water to loaded water fixtures across that building, including other rooms and floors.
description=[*] Whole-Building Water depends on Water Supply; disabling normal supply disables the building connection too.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Infinite Native Power[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Enable [b]Power[/b] and vanilla refrigerators, lights, televisions and other electrical devices use the game's native electricity check.
description=[*] Horizontal and vertical coverage follows the world's vanilla generator-range sandbox settings.
description=[*] Power is infinite and silent: no fuel, wiring, noise, fumes or visible generator maintenance.
description=[*] Dense overlapping powered pipes are compressed per loaded chunk while preserving the same powered tiles.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Optional Area Cleanup — Read Before Enabling[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] The cleanup switch is hidden by default. Enable [b]Show the Area Cleanup pipe switch[/b] in Sandbox or Mod Options to expose it.
description=[*] Cleanup categories are configurable: weeds/removable vegetation, trees, living zombies, zombie corpses, blood and dropped ground items. Planted crops and animal corpses are preserved.
description=[*] [b]Warning:[/b] cleanup is destructive. The Ground Items category permanently deletes every dropped item within enabled pipe ranges.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Performance, Multiplayer and Saves[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] No permanent per-frame crop, appliance or whole-building scan. Maintenance is low-frequency; placement, planting, harvesting and setting changes use targeted immediate paths.
description=[*] Large coverage maps and building scans are processed in small bounded batches to avoid one large frame spike.
description=[*] Supports Build 42 single-player and multiplayer. Placement, removal, claims and setting changes are server-authoritative and distance-validated.
description=[*] Compatible with existing saves, vanilla crops and most modded crops registered through the standard farming system.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Settings and Languages[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] World settings are available in Sandbox and mirrored in in-game Mod Options. Single-player can edit them in-game; multiplayer clients are read-only because the server owns them.
description=[*] The floor-rendering mode is a local visual option and does not affect the server or simulation.
description=[*] English, Simplified Chinese and Russian are fully supported.
description=[/list]
